FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

  • What services do you offer?

    Briefly list your main services and who they are for.

  • Do I need to book in advance?

    Explain how to book — phone, LINE or the contact form — and walk-in policy.

  • How much does it cost?

    Give a price range or explain how pricing is decided.

  • Which payment methods do you accept?

    Cash, bank transfer, credit card, QR payment …

  • How long does it take?

    Typical time for a first visit or project.

  • Is there parking?

    Parking details or nearby public transport.
